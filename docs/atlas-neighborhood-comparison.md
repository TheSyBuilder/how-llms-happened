# Local neighborhoods: comparison protocol

**Status:** bounded static prototype for evaluation. `/atlas/cases/beginner/` and `/atlas/cases/expert/` colocate four existing inspection cases with complete incident lists and optional SVG diagrams. The catalogue remains the baseline. No learner comparison has been conducted; the diagram is not required navigation and no learning benefit is claimed.

The expert [evidence-lens companion](atlas-evidence-lens-comparison.md) compares precomputed subsets over the same four neighborhoods, with complete incident evidence. It defines independent field intersections and count scopes before any client filtering; its acceptance does not promote the diagram.

This implements the next prototype step in the [atlas overview brief](atlas-overview.md). It changes no corpus claim, date, relationship or availability. All factual answers below are drawn from that brief's four grounded records and their bound primary evidence.

## Design and interaction contract

The product job is to help beginners distinguish public chronology from influence and to help experts audit the scope of a connection. The primary action is to inspect a named relationship and its date/version evidence. The north star remains an annotated research index: the existing warm ledger owns typography, color, rules, density and native disclosures. [Connected Papers](https://www.connectedpapers.com/about) contributes only focus on a local neighborhood; geometric similarity and inferred connections are excluded. Its JavaScript-only page supplied no new inspectable content on 2026-10-11; the earlier first-party audit remains the reference.

The exact learning objective is to explain what one incident record establishes while independently identifying its ordering event, canonical version, evidential basis and causal permission. A static list is already sufficient to answer the task. The optional diagram tests whether a shared focus and adjacent branches help a reader locate the relevant record among competing connections. Drawing lines alone does not establish educational value.

Readers choose a case through native anchors, optionally open its diagram, predict or audit a claim, then open relationship evidence. The visual response is a disclosure, with no recomputation of history or camera movement. Beginner mode presents prediction and bounded explanation before the list. Expert mode presents an event/version and source-locator audit, without revealing the answer in advance. The same work and relationship records underlie both paths; case-specific mode links preserve the case anchor.

Each diagram contains the focus and exactly one equal-size mark per distinct incident neighbor. All parallel relationship records remain separate paths and separate evidence disclosures. Textual R-numbers connect paths to their HTML records; they are local reading labels, not historical identifiers or ranks. Only qualified records have arrowheads, respecting stored orientation. A prohibited record has none, including the documented Graves comparison. Solid, dotted and dashed strokes encode documented influence, inherited mechanism and editorial synthesis respectively. The full evidence class and causal restriction are independently printed in HTML. Neighbor order follows first appearance in the corpus relationship list; it never asserts chronology. No connection is drawn between neighbors, even if one exists elsewhere in the corpus.

The diagram is a redundant, non-focusable illustration. The adjacent caption names its scope and points to the structured list; all facts remain available when graphics are hidden. This follows [WAI's complex-image guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/complex/), accessed 2026-10-11, which calls for accessible structured alternatives. Native links and disclosures support keyboard and JavaScript-off access. The SVG is omitted below the desktop breakpoint rather than shrinking text; mobile keeps the same lists, tasks, dates and evidence. Reduced motion needs no alternate animation. Print reveals evidence. No graph library, client JavaScript, remote data request or new dependency is needed.

## Tasks and error criteria

Use the companion page with its diagram closed as condition A and the same page with its diagram open as condition B. Keep task copy, date crosswalks, evidence placement and source links identical so the comparison isolates the diagram. The complete catalogue/evidence routes remain the navigation baseline; assess their lookup cost separately rather than attributing colocation benefits to the drawing. Provide the same work, record ID and question in both conditions. Counterbalance condition order and case order to reduce practice effects. For the beginner task, record the prediction before revealing the explanation. For expert inspection, require the locator and version as part of the answer. Do not compare beginner and expert completion times as though their tasks were equivalent.

| Case and task | Correct observation | Material error |
|---|---|---|
| Markov–Shannon: what does this opening connection establish? | An editorial conceptual parallel and teaching boundary; reviewed evidence does not document direct influence. Markov's printed calendar note stays unresolved. | Claiming a proven influence chain, a Gregorian conversion, or that absent evidence proves there was no influence. |
| Elman–Mikolov: which date orders Elman, and what carries into the word model? | April 1988 report orders the work; March 1990 identifies the canonical article. Named Elman architecture/citation supports copied state and context dependence within recorded scope. | Replacing the ordering date with 1990, fabricating a day, or transferring the training procedure, datasets or results. |
| Graves–Bahdanau: does documented comparison establish derivation? | No. Inspect Bahdanau v1 article p. 9 or v7 p. 8, section 6.1: the monotonic-window comparison is documented and causal wording remains prohibited. | Treating a solid line or documented-influence class as proof of inspiration or derivation. |
| Cho–Sutskever: what do June and September posting dates establish? | Public posting order only; parallel-development/independent-strategy evidence is bound to Sutskever v1. Conception order and priority remain unestablished. | Claiming conception priority, direct derivation, or attributing the v1 concurrency wording to later revisions. |

For each condition, record answer correctness, material-error category, success reaching the matching source locator, assistance needed, and time from task presentation to the source. Record whether the reader can distinguish incident relationship count from unique-neighbor count and explain why no transitive path is warranted. Note confusion introduced by geometry, arrows, R-numbers, evidence classes or dates. Preserve only anonymized aggregate results in public documentation, with participant consent; no study results exist yet.

The prototype passes technical acceptance when every task is reachable with native keyboard and JavaScript off; all nodes, parallel records, orientation and evidence match the projection; missing/isolated work handling is explicit; date precision and calendar remain unchanged; mode links preserve the case; graphics-hidden and print treatments retain all information; expanded axe checks and 1440/768/640/320 reflow pass; and routes ship zero client JavaScript within the atlas HTML budget.

Promotion into catalogue navigation requires a separate learner comparison. Report sample, procedure and limitations rather than inventing a statistical threshold. Retain the static treatment if the diagram introduces any of the material errors above or offers no useful inspection advantage. A faster lookup alone cannot compensate for worse factual interpretation. No global graph, transitive path, filter lens or recurrent cursor is accepted by this prototype.

## Data boundary

The incident projection retains the original atlas work and relationship objects, groups distinct neighbors without merging their records, and derives incident, neighbor and outside-neighborhood counts. Missing work IDs fail explicitly; an isolated admitted work yields empty neighbors and records. It does not recursively expand. Focus and neighbor dates keep the raw ordering event and full canonical identity. The HTML crosswalk exposes event precision, calendar, notes and date-evidence links; each relationship keeps claim bindings, all source locators/support and uncertainties through the shared evidence component.

Factual sources remain the [corpus](../data/corpus.json) and its version-bound register. Targeted checks of [Cho's arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.1078) and [Sutskever v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.3215v1) on 2026-10-11 confirmed the June/September posting boundary. This is not a new endpoint survey or a full re-audit of all four cases. Source access dates in the corpus are unchanged.
